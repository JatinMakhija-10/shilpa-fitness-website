WEBSITE AUDIT & REVAMP RECOMMENDATION
Shilpa K's Pilates Studio
Professional review of the current website covering positioning, UX, conversion, credibility, content, local SEO
and visual direction.
Overall assessment
~5/10
Audit 1
4.5 / 10
Audit 2
5.5 / 10
Primary opportunity
Turn the website from an information-led site into a trust-building, conversion-focused
studio experience.
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The current website has the right basic ingredients: the studio, services, pricing, Shilpa, testimonials, gallery and contact
pathways are present. The issue is how those ingredients are presented.
Across both supplied audits, the consistent finding is that the website communicates what the studio offers, but does not
consistently communicate why a visitor should choose Shilpa. The result is a site that informs rather than strongly
persuades.
The revamp should therefore not be treated as a cosmetic redesign. It should improve
positioning, trust, proof, service depth, pricing clarity, local discoverability and the path from
first visit to trial booking.

01 HERO / FIRST IMPRESSION
The hero is clear, but not distinctive enough to create a
strong reason to choose the studio.
KEY ISSUES
• The headline “Transform Your Body & Confidence with Pilates & Face Yoga” is broad and interchangeable with many
wellness businesses.
• The current imagery makes the studio tangible, but the hero does not yet strongly show Shilpa or a real client
experience.
• The audits identify weak CTA hierarchy and limited immediate proof such as verified ratings, certifications, experience
or client count.
• The hero contains more explanatory copy than necessary before establishing the strongest brand message.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Rewrite the headline around a specific customer, outcome and point of difference.
• Make BOOK A TRIAL the single dominant CTA and keep WhatsApp secondary.
• Add a compact, verified trust strip beneath the primary action.
• Use real coaching/client imagery where possible.
02 AUDIENCE & POSITIONING
The website tries to speak to too many customer
situations at once.
KEY ISSUES
• The audience section covers beginners, working professionals, mothers, pregnancy, pain, people who dislike gyms and
Face Yoga customers.
• The broad targeting weakens the sense that the studio is specifically designed for a particular customer.
• Repeated card layouts make the experience feel more like a template than a distinctive brand.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Prioritise 3–4 core customer situations.
• Write directly to their needs, concerns and desired experience.
• Replace repeated cards with stronger editorial layouts and photography.
03 SERVICES
The services are understandable, but they are described
rather than strongly sold.
KEY ISSUES
• Pilates, Rehab, Prenatal, Face Yoga and Nutrition are presented with similar visual treatment despite having different
customer needs.
• The audits identify generic wellness language such as “natural glow”, “sustainable results” and “feel your best”.
• Movement imagery and examples of the actual experience are limited.

04 PRICING & PLANS
Pricing is one of the clearest conversion opportunities.
KEY ISSUES
• The audits found that plan names can be understood without making the full decision easy.
• Monthly Group, 3-Month, Semi-Private and 1-to-1 options need clearer comparison.
• There is no obvious recommended package in the supplied material.
• Visitors should not have to work out what each plan includes before they can decide.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Show price, sessions, group size, inclusions and validity clearly where appropriate.
• Highlight a recommended plan.
• Connect each relevant plan to BOOK A TRIAL.
• If pricing is enquiry-only, explain the process clearly rather than leaving ambiguity.
05 MEET SHILPA
This should be one of the strongest trust sections on the
entire website.
KEY ISSUES
• The latest version has improved by introducing a real photograph of Shilpa.
• However, the audits still identify limited visibility of certifications, experience and specialisations.
• The biography does not yet communicate enough of Shilpa’s story, teaching philosophy or methodology.
• A personal coaching image would communicate more than a static portrait alone.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Make Shilpa the central differentiator.
• Show verified credentials, experience and specialisations.
• Use an authentic coaching image.
• Explain why she teaches, how she approaches clients and what makes her method different.
06 TESTIMONIALS & SOCIAL PROOF
The site asks visitors to trust the testimonials more than
it proves them.
KEY ISSUES
• The audits flag text-based testimonials without enough visible review-source context.
• The phrase “Real Results from Real Clients” needs evidence that feels equally real.
• There is not enough visible trust stacking through ratings, review count, credentials and specific client stories.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Use genuine Google review excerpts where available.
• Add source, rating and date where appropriate.

07 GALLERY & CLASS EXPERIENCE
The current gallery is a useful improvement, but it
should sell the experience rather than only the room.
KEY ISSUES
• The latest material shows the physical studio and equipment more effectively.
• The audits still identify an experience gap: a beginner cannot immediately understand what their first session feels like.
• Most visual emphasis should move from equipment alone toward people, coaching, movement and atmosphere.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Add a “Your First Session” 3–4 step journey.
• Show Shilpa coaching, small groups, real movement and client interaction.
• Use real photography as proof, not decoration.
08 BOOKING & CTA SYSTEM
The website makes the visitor choose between too many
versions of the same action.
KEY ISSUES
• The audit identifies variations such as Book a Trial, Book Your Trial, Book Trial, Check Availability, Chat Now and
WhatsApp Now.
• Multiple CTA labels dilute the primary action.
• The enquiry flow can ask for more information than is necessary.
• The visitor is not given enough reassurance about what happens after submitting.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Use one primary CTA throughout: BOOK A TRIAL.
• Keep WhatsApp secondary.
• Shorten the form to essential information.
• Explain the next step after submission.
09 CONTACT & LOCAL SEO
The website has local intent, but it can work much
harder for local discovery and conversion.
KEY ISSUES
• Goregaon, Malad and Kandivali are mentioned, giving the site a useful local foundation.
• The audit recommends more exact location/landmark information, Google Maps and stronger local landing-page depth.
• Operating/batch information and local conversion details should be easier to find.
• Educational content is limited, reducing the site's ability to capture relevant search demand.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Add exact location/landmark and Google Maps.

10 GLOBAL DESIGN & BRAND
The visual system is clean, but repetitive and too
template-driven.
KEY ISSUES
• The audits repeatedly identify similar rounded cards, similar spacing and similar visual treatment across sections.
• Visual hierarchy is not strong enough: important conversion moments should feel more important than supporting
information.
• Some whitespace reads as unfinished rather than intentional.
• The site needs a stronger editorial rhythm and more real photography.
RECOMMENDED DIRECTION
• Reduce the number of card-based sections.
• Use scale, photography, typography and composition to create hierarchy.
• Keep whitespace purposeful.
• Build a visual identity around the real studio and Shilpa rather than generic wellness patterns.

13 — FINAL RECOMMENDATION
This is not a “make it prettier” project.
The current website already contains enough information to establish the business. The missing layer is the customer
journey: a compelling reason to choose Shilpa, visible proof, a clear first-session experience, confident pricing and an
effortless booking path.
The strongest opportunity is to turn the website into a local trust-and-lead-generation asset rather than a static service
catalogue.
THE STANDARD THE NEW SITE SHOULD MEET
A visitor should be able to answer six questions quickly:
• Is this studio for someone like me?
• Why should I choose Shilpa specifically?
• Can I trust her expertise?
• What will my first session actually be like?
• Which plan or service makes sense for me?
• How do I book a trial right now?
